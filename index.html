Most of us have stories we can no longer quite remember: a first job, how they met, life in another decade, the sound of a laugh. Writing a memoir takes months, and typing on a phone is hard for many people. This makes it as easy as picking up the phone.

How it works

1

A weekly 10-minute call

We ring you, or your relative, on a mobile or landline at a time that suits. No app needed.

2

Warm, personal questions

Our AI asks about life and memories, and follows the thread of what is said.

3

A family archive

Every call is transcribed and sorted by decade into a private archive your family can read anytime. Recordings are kept only if you choose to keep them.

The more you share, the more your family keeps.

Ten minutes a week adds up to roughly 8 hours of stories a year. Start now, and in twenty years your family has a library of your life, told in your own words.
