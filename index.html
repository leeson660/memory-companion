I wish I'd recorded their voice while I could.

Most of us have stories we can no longer quite remember: a first job, how they met, life in another decade, the sound of a laugh. Writing a memoir takes months, and typing on a phone is hard for many people. This makes it as easy as picking up the phone.

How it works

1

A weekly 10-minute call

We ring you or your relative on a mobile or landline at a time that suits. No app or setup required for calls.

2

Warm, personal questions

Our AI asks about life and memories, following the thread of what is said.

3

A private family web archive

Access your private web dashboard on any device to read formatted transcripts, listen to audio recordings (if enabled), and view stories sorted by decade. Recordings are kept only if you choose to keep them.

The more you share, the more your family keeps.

Ten minutes a week adds up to roughly 8 hours of stories a year. Start now, and in twenty years your family has a library of your life, told in your own words.

Coming later: turn your stories into a printed keepsake book, with chapters from your life, to treasure and pass down.
